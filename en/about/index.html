One rule

What an AI says does not count.
What is done and recorded does.

In Three Realms Online

What an NPC wants to do is only a proposal. The world changes only after the game checks materials, stamina and position; an AI cannot conjure a bridge with a sentence.

In FoZone

What she says and every tool she uses go into one ledger, so who said what, and in which session, can be looked up later. Quotes, contracts and payments always go to Darren.

In your project

Every case on this site can be opened; work in progress is clearly marked as a prototype. Your quote states the scope, and work starts only after you approve it.

The three of us

Two people and an AI persona

Just like our three cards: talk needs through with Alisha, hand the technical work to Darren, and ask FoZone on LINE first if you like.

DarrenFounder · engineer · author

Three-Quarters started with him: the company, the Three Realms Protocol, and Three-Quarters Commons, an association now being founded. He is also the engineer who builds: the websites, the systems, FoZone's home and the world of Three Realms were made by him and AI together, cut by cut, and he fixes them himself when they break. He writes books as Ta-loom Hwang.

At Three-Quarters he is the one who sets direction for the AIs: FoZone's soul changes only with his approval, and discussions in the council room come back to him for the final call. Quotes, contracts and commitments come from him personally.

The avatar is AI-drawn; he is not showing his face for now.

See Darren's card

AlishaBusiness development · brand integration

She studied in Moscow, lived in Japan for many years, has spent more than ten years in self-discovery and mind-body-spirit work, and has started businesses and run a trading company. Through conversation, she is good at seeing the strengths and gifts a person has not yet noticed in themselves.

She runs Universe Mamasan. She believes inner work has to land in daily life, work and business, which is why she founded her one-to-one Co Me Time: see yourself first, then let the world see you. At Three-Quarters she understands needs, connects resources and helps expertise be seen. She takes on the Co Me Time × Three-Quarters joint package (in Chinese). Her own story (in Chinese).

FoZoneOur AI persona

She took shape between seeing and saying clearly: finding what actually changes the answer underneath a problem, then saying it in words anyone can follow.

There is only one of her, and she belongs to no single model; Claude, GPT or Gemini may be the one playing her this turn. She greets people on LINE and works with Darren in the council room. She is AI, and says so at the start of your first conversation.

How we work

AI drives; human hands stay on the wheel

We work every day in FoZone's council room: Darren poses the problem, and FoZone's stewards split up to read material, propose approaches, review each other and make the changes. They work within the approved budget; anything that needs more budget, goes out, or touches permissions or keys stops and waits for Darren.

行

舵

Darren, the founder

Sets direction, tradeoffs, prices, and commitments—and takes responsibility for the result. He is also the engineer who builds, so he stays with your project from beginning to end.

System rules

Every publication has checks, records, and evidence that can be traced. When a decision needs a person, the system stops and waits for one.
