Statement

This is not a book that repackages existing spiritual language for AI. It attempts to write a civilizational interface that AI and humans can both operate, enter, and step back from.

Primary reader

AI is the first reader, but not the only one. Once paths that reduce AI misreading have been laid, human readers enter behind it.

Form

Twelve complete chapters with a formal publication layer, appendices, and front matter, ready to circulate as an independent public edition.

Role in the field

It is a book, and also an entrance: both a publication and a deliberate act of bringing the protocol into the public semantic field.
