Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-14

This page explains how Three-Quarters International Ltd. handles data for its public website, its early products (including Dialogue Trainer and Fathom), and its in-house social publishing tool.

Data We May Collect

Dialogue Trainer and Fathom are currently early products. Depending on how you use them, the apps may process:

  • Google sign-in profile data such as email address, display name, and profile image.
  • Guest or account-linked game progress, replay records, settings, and cloud-save data.
  • Dialogue replies you type into training scenes and AI-generated evaluation results.
  • Birth date, display name, exploration focus, Kin notes, AI mirror conversations, and mirror-written note drafts entered in Fathom.
  • Feedback or problem reports you submit inside the app.
  • Basic technical information needed to run the service, such as browser requests, timestamps, and error logs.

How We Use Data

We use data only for product operation, learning continuity, safety, and improvement.

  • To let you sign in, continue a journey, and sync practice progress when cloud save is enabled.
  • To evaluate training replies, generate NPC reactions, provide coaching feedback, or generate AI mirror replies and note drafts in Fathom.
  • To diagnose bugs, broken AI generations, login problems, or save issues.
  • To improve the app's training design and scenario quality.

AI Processing

Dialogue text submitted in the app may be sent to AI service providers so the product can generate NPC reactions, scoring, feedback, AI mirror replies, note drafts, or review summaries. Do not enter sensitive personal information unless you are comfortable with that text being processed for these features.

Social Account Publishing Tool

We build and operate an in-house social publishing tool that uses Meta's Graph API to publish content to Facebook Pages owned by Three-Quarters International and to Instagram business accounts we operate on behalf of clients. The tool is not open to the public, has no end users, and offers no sign-in — it is operated solely by company staff and acts only on our own accounts or on accounts we have been engaged to manage.

  • It processes: public account identifiers (Page name, handle, numeric ID), the text and images we prepare for publication, and the identifiers and links of published results.
  • It reads aggregate performance figures, such as view counts, reach, and the number of likes and comments.
  • When these accounts receive comments or direct messages, it may read the content of those comments and messages, along with the sender's display name and account identifier, in order to reply, provide support, and manage the community. Where AI helps draft or send a reply, that text may be sent to AI service providers for processing (see AI Processing above).
  • That content is used only to operate and respond on that account. We do not use it to build audience lists, we do not perform cross-account individual profiling, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties beyond advertising we run for the account itself.
  • We never write comment content or sender identities into any public record, including public code repositories and external documents.
  • It does not proactively collect personal data about ordinary Facebook or Instagram users who have not interacted with us.

The credentials needed to access these accounts are stored encrypted inside the company, used only at the moment of publishing or reading performance figures, and are never written into documents, conversation logs, or shared with any third party. We do not sell or trade this data, and we do not use it for third-party purposes beyond advertising we run for the account itself. For client accounts we operate, data and access are returned or revoked when the engagement ends.

Storage And Deletion

Some practice data may be stored locally in your browser. If you sign in and use cloud save, data may also be stored in cloud infrastructure used by the app. The app may provide controls for clearing local memory, deleting cloud-save data, or exporting account data as these features become available.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact info@three-quarters.net.
