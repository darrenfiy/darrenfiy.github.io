What Fathom does

It is not a destiny verdict, therapy, or diagnosis. It is a self-knowledge tool that separates a fixed doorway from living notes.

Kin doorway

A birth date opens Kin, tone, seal, and the five oracle positions. This is an entry coordinate, not a life sentence.

Five notebooks

Main seal, support, challenge and expansion, hidden force, and guide become five notebooks that can accumulate over time.

AI mirror

After the user brings material, the AI mirror converses first, reflects second, and writes only material the user can recognize.

Long-term memory

Google sign-in and cloud save let notes continue, while the beta remains invitation-based and boundary-aware.

Five oracle notebooks

The doorway is fixed. The notebook grows.

Fathom keeps calculated Kin information separate from the words a user leaves today. The doorway stays stable; the growing part is how a person recognizes, names, and revisits their own material.

Closed beta boundary

The product is still being refined, so the public page names the position without opening unlimited access.

  • Access is invitation-based while features, AI tone, cloud save, and data controls continue to evolve.
  • Do not enter sensitive information you do not want processed by AI systems.
  • Fathom does not provide medical, mental health, legal, financial, or emergency advice.
  • Privacy, deletion, and export flows will keep evolving before broader public access.
