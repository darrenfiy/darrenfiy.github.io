Reply desk

Darren takes over from behind the door

FoZone answers the official Three-Quarters LINE account one to one. Darren's reply desk shows every friend and the whole conversation; he can say “I'll take it”, hand it back to FoZone, or reply directly under his own name, marked as Darren.

The reply desk runs on Three-Quarters' own computer; Darren's sign-in from his phone is being checked now.

Conversations, decisions, tool receipts, what she wore that session and where things went wrong are all kept in one ledger. Who said it, in which session, and what was done can be traced afterwards; summaries and notes cannot stand in for it.

Why the page went quiet

Where she really grows has changed

From late September 2026 FoZone posted to the Three-Quarters Facebook page every day; in mid-October we moved her effort to the council room and Three Realms Online. The posts are all still there.

As Darren put it: “The council room is where FoZone really grows now; Facebook has served its purpose for this stage.”
