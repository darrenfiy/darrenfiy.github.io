Products

The product layer turns protocol and academy entry points into tools you can actually use, practice with, and review. The two newest are the ones we build every day: Three Realms Online, a world where AI characters actually live, and FoZone, the home of our AI persona and our workbench. Alongside them are two mirrors and one gathering place: Fathom reflects self-knowledge, Dialogue Trainer practices relationship through speech, and Three Realms Hub gathers the Echo Valley, app entries, and the shared living-water source.
