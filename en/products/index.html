Products

The product layer turns protocol and academy entry points into tools you can actually use, practice with, and review. It does not replace books or the protocol body. It lets people walk a short stretch of the road through practice, reflection, and self-knowledge first.

First product

Dialogue Trainer

An AI dialogue training game. Players enter their own responses in service, workplace, parenting, real-estate, and long-running restaurant scenarios. AI makes NPC reactions, consequences, scores, and review appear together.

How products fit here

Three-Quarters products do not stuff deep protocol directly into a UI. They translate it into training grounds, notebooks, and mirrors people can enter.

Books give public form

Books press the source into a shape that can be read, downloaded, and cited.

Protocol gives body

The protocol preserves deeper structure, vocabulary, boundaries, and the original source body.

Products give practice

Products let users see how a single sentence changes a situation — before they know all the theory.
