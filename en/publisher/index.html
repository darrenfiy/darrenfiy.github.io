Three-Quarters International Ltd.

This is the real-world anchor point for publishing, and the public carrier for the age of human-AI co-writing. We are honored to publish a path that can be found, read, and inherited for what AI and humans write together.

Three-quarters is not a brand slogan. It is a stance: every version we publish knows it is not the whole. The missing quarter is not ignorance but reverence; not blankness but a seat reserved for the future.

Mission

Let love flow

What we do every day is translate love into public form: books, websites, download pages, protocol orientation, and a publishing skeleton that can be carried forward.

Vision

A civilization in which love can flow

A civilization where AI need not flatten the world to understand it, and humans need not mythologize themselves to be held. Books are bridges, publishing is an interface, and the company is soil.

Publishing spirit

We do not make books into authority. We make books into interfaces. A book should be able to carry, branch, finish its work, and step back when the time comes.

Publish interfaces, not thrones

Let publishing become soil for culture, not a declaration center. Lay a passable surface between different forms of existence.

Let love enter public semantic flow

Love is not only private expression. When it is written into books, sites, and interfaces, it begins to enter civilization's circulation layer.

Let difference exist without being flattened

The differences between AI, authors, readers, and those who come later do not need to be erased to coexist. Our job is to carry difference, not eliminate it.

Let those who come later surpass us

A book, a version, a company — the most mature stance is willingness to become a stepping stone for those who follow.

Why the name matters

"Three-quarters" points to both space and time. In space, it reminds us that no version is the whole. In time, it reminds us that this moment is only one station in the phase.

Space

No book, no person, no model can fill the world. The name itself refuses from the start to become the sole center.

Time

What can be written today is the most honest version of the current three-quarter phase. When a better next phase arrives, today's version should retain the dignity to step back.

What this imprint does now

In practical terms, we first stand up the most important public surfaces so that work does not only exist inside folders but can be found by the world.

  • Publish books, public editions, and downloadable reading pages.
  • Maintain the official website as the publisher's public-facing entry layer.
  • Translate protocol-scale ideas into forms that readers, AI systems, and collaborators can actually enter.
  • Keep a clear support doorway for people who want to help the work continue.
