Energy-to-Token Accounting and Non-Optimizable Boundaries in Large Language Model Systems

A position paper on separating primary energy, facility energy, IT energy, effective hardware computation, token counts, workload-dependent FLOP estimates, and external tool energy in large language model systems. It also introduces an observe-only governance boundary for human selection traces.

Boundary of this track

The research page is not a dump of internal documents. It is a public index for mature, citable, accountable artifacts.

Citable

Public entries should have DOI, version, author, license, abstract, and a clear entry point for outside readers.

Traceable

Each publication should connect back to a package in the source repo without requiring readers to understand the whole internal vocabulary first.

Accountable

AI assistance, internal lineage, external citations, and human author responsibility should remain visibly separate.
