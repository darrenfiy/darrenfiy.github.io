Quotes

How quotes work

Reference prices are a baseline for a typical scope. After discussing your needs, we provide a written quote with the scope stated clearly.

One-time builds include two rounds of revisions within scope. Additional pages, features, or substantial rewrites are quoted separately.

Domains, hosting, LINE message fees, AI usage, payment-processing fees, paid assets, photography, and advertising are separate.

Payment terms and installments are stated in each quote.

You provide the text, photos, and assets used on the site, or confirm that they may be used.

Only already-public pages are used as public case studies; we ask before sharing anything else.

Who takes the work: Three-Quarters International Ltd. is currently registered as dormant. At this stage, founder Darren accepts projects in his personal capacity. The quote will explain how payment and documentation are handled, in accordance with the laws applicable when the work is accepted.
