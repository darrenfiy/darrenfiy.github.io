Walk with us

Three-Quarters is weaving a path from writing, protocol, and books into tools people can actually use. The first product is Dialogue Trainer: an AI dialogue training game that lets people practice saying the next right thing under pressure.

If you want to support this, you are not buying into a finished myth. You are walking alongside a path that is still being generated. You can try the product, introduce a tester, discuss collaboration, or buy Darren a coffee to keep the writing and development going.
